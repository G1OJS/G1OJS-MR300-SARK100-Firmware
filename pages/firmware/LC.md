As in the existing V13 firmeware, inductance and capacitance can be measured by selecting the appropriate mode via the MODE key. In the G1OJS version, the calculations have been rewritten so that they work above 10MHz, and the value of R measured is also displayed on the screen. Note that care must be taken when measuring these components as they are very likely to have a self resonance within the HF bands and measuring close to this or above it will very likely lead to erroneous results.
